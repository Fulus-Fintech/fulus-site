What Fulus is

Fulus is record-keeping software for private investment clubs. It records contributions and withdrawals, tracks what a club holds, and calculates each member's share.

Fulus is not a bank, a broker, a fund, or a licensed financial institution. We do not hold your money, move it, invest it, or execute trades. Every transfer happens between you and your club through your own bank, outside this app; Fulus records what you and your club tell it.
