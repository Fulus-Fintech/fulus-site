Privacy

This page covers the fulus.sa waitlist. Plain language, short on purpose.

What we store

Your email address, your language preference, and — if you answer the optional questions — whether you have a club and how big it is. That’s the whole list.

Why we store it

To hold your place in line, to send you launch updates, and to invite you when your wave opens. Your email is never sold or shared with anyone.

Want off the list?

Send an email from the address you signed up with and we’ll delete your record. Reach us at hello@fulus.sa.

We handle this data in line with the principles of Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
