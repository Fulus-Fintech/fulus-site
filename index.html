01

Pool the money, without the chasing.

Every member’s contribution lands in one shared record — who paid, when, and the receipt to prove it. No more screenshots forwarded three times.

02

Decide together, on the record.

Proposals get a card, members get a vote, and the outcome is stored where everyone can see it — not buried in a group chat.

Fulus computes each member’s ownership from their actual contributions, every cycle. No estimates, no awkward conversations.
